Fluid particle interaction:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_%28physics%29 <-- Low Reynolds section

Fd = 6 * pi * mu * r * (u - v)
Fd  = drag force acting on the particle
mu  = dynamic viscosity of the fluid
r   = particle radius
u   = local fluid velocity
v   = particle velocity
pi  = 3.14159265

If the particle goes faster the fluid slows it down, if it goes fast it pushes it

Schema of required and possible kernels made with LLM (not all the physics already verified):
1. lbm_collision_kernel
   - Computes density (rho) and velocity (u) in each lattice cell.
   - Evaluates the equilibrium distributions feq.
   - Performs the BGK collision step:
     f_i* = f_i - (dt/tau) * (f_i - feq_i)

2. lbm_streaming_kernel
   - Propagates post-collision distributions to neighboring cells
     along the D3Q19 directions.

3. lbm_boundary_kernel
   - Applies vessel wall boundary conditions.
   - Typically uses bounce-back to enforce the no-slip condition.

4. lbm_macroscopic_kernel
   - Reconstructs macroscopic quantities from distributions:
       rho = sum(f_i)
       u   = (1/rho) * sum(f_i * c_i)
   - Stores rho and u for later use.

5. reset_particle_forces_kernel
   - Resets forces and torques on all particles:
       F = 0
       T = 0

6. interpolate_fluid_to_particles_kernel
   - Interpolates fluid quantities from the LBM grid
     to each particle center.
   - Retrieves:
       u_fluid
       Omega_fluid (optional)

7. particle_drag_kernel
   - Computes fluid-particle interaction forces.
   - Example:
       F_drag = 6*pi*mu*r*(u_fluid - v)
   - Computes hydrodynamic torque if particle rotation is enabled.

8. reset_cell_list_kernel
   - Clears spatial partitioning structures.
   - Prepares cell lists for neighbor search.

9. assign_particles_to_cells_kernel
   - Assigns each particle to its spatial cell.
   - Enables efficient local collision detection.

10. particle_contact_kernel
    - Detects particle-particle contacts.
    - Computes overlap, contact normal and relative velocity.
    - Applies spring-damper forces:
        F_n = (k*delta - gamma*v_n)*n
    - Computes tangential friction forces and torques.

11. wall_contact_kernel
    - Detects contacts between particles and vessel walls.
    - Applies repulsive spring-damper wall forces.
    - Computes wall-induced torques when needed.

12. lbm_force_coupling_kernel
    - Applies reaction forces to the fluid.
    - Ensures momentum conservation:
        F_fluid = -F_particle

13. update_velocity_kernel
    - Integrates translational motion:
        v = v + dt*F/m

14. update_position_kernel
    - Updates particle positions:
        x = x + dt*v

15. update_angular_velocity_kernel
    - Integrates rotational motion:
        omega = omega + dt*T/I

16. update_orientation_kernel
    - Updates particle orientation.
    - Typically integrates quaternions:
        q_dot = 0.5 * quat(0,omega) * q
        q = normalize(q + dt*q_dot)

17. update_shape_matrix_kernel
    - Updates ellipsoid geometry information.
    - Recomputes:
        A = R * diag(1/a²,1/b²,1/c²) * R^T
    - Used for ellipsoid contact detection.

18. output_statistics_kernel
    - Computes diagnostic quantities:
        total mass
        kinetic energy
        average velocity
        collision counts
    - Prepares VTK/CSV output every N timesteps.
